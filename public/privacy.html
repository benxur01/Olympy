2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to operate the platform.

2.1. Information you provide

  • Account data: phone number (primary identifier), full name, optional username, password (stored hashed).
  • Profile: avatar image, subjects, grade/level, learning goals, onboarding preferences.
  • Telegram linkage: Telegram user/chat IDs for phone verification, password reset, and notifications.
  • Learning activity: olympiad/test attempts, answers, scores, rankings, certificates, practice results, code submissions.
  • Organization data: education center details, memberships (student, teacher, manager, owner), center images.
  • Payments: subscription plan, amount, status, and provider transaction IDs (Click / Payme). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Support: AI assistant and admin support chat messages.
  • Optional 2FA: TOTP secrets stored encrypted.

2.2. Information collected automatically

  • Authentication cookies (JWT) and local/session storage for sessions, tokens, and temporary test state.
  • IP address: your IP address is not merely used in passing — it is stored in our database: with every successful sign-in record (IP address, device/browser string, and time — your “login history”), in the administrative audit log, in security flags (for example when many accounts are seen behind one IP), and in the list of blocked IP addresses/networks. Failed sign-in attempts may also be recorded together with the IP address. These records are visible only to the platform administrator during a security review.
  • Why we need the IP address: security and anti-abuse only — detecting brute-force and account-takeover attempts, rate limiting, investigating fraud and multi-account abuse, and blocking an IP address or network when necessary. We do not derive your precise location from the IP address, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not sell it to third parties.
  • Security logs: unauthorized and rate-limited requests are written to our server logs together with the IP address.
  • Error monitoring (Sentry): limited device/browser diagnostics.
  • Usage metrics: streaks, coins, activity dates, and aggregate analytics for admin/center dashboards.

2.3. Camera and microphone (exam proctoring)

For some olympiads the organizer may enable exam proctoring. It consists of two independent features — webcam monitoring and microphone (voice) monitoring — each enabled separately. Both are off by default: in exams without proctoring, the camera and microphone are never requested.

Most important: no video, image, audio recording, or voice sample ever leaves your device. We do not transmit, store, or show them to anyone. There is no recording functionality at all.

  • Consent first: before the camera or microphone is requested, a separate consent screen explains why it is needed. If you decline, the camera/microphone is never activated. The only thing stored on our servers is a “consent given” flag and a timestamp.
  • How it works: camera frames and the microphone signal are analysed in real time entirely on your device (in browser memory, inside a dedicated Web Worker) and discarded immediately. For the camera we detect face presence, the number of faces, and sustained gaze direction; for the microphone we measure only sound energy — speech content is not recognised, nothing is transcribed, and no voice-based identification is performed.
  • What reaches our servers: only a short signal name — for example no_face_detected, multiple_faces_detected, gaze_away_sustained, ambient_speech_detected. This string is attached to your exam attempt record and is visible to the manager/teacher of the education center that organised the exam.
  • Purpose: exam integrity only. These signals are never used for advertising, profiling, or biometric identification, and are not shared with third parties.
  • Withdrawing permission: you can revoke camera and microphone permission at any time in your browser or device settings. You may then be unable to take exams that require proctoring, but every other part of the platform continues to work normally.
