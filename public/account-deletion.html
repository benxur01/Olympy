1. Summary

App: Olympy (Prolymp) — prolymp.uz

Who deletes it: the account owner, from the Profile section of the app (“Danger zone”), confirmed with the account password.

Restore window: 30 days. After that, the data is permanently deleted.

If you cannot sign in: sanjarruzmetov017@gmail.com

You do not need to enter anything on this page and this page never asks you to sign in — it only explains the actual deletion path.

2. Deleting from inside the app (main path)

  1. Sign in to Olympy — prolymp.uz or the mobile app.
  2. Open the Profile section from the menu.
  3. Scroll to the “Danger zone” block at the bottom of the page and tap “Delete account”.
  4. Enter your current password in the dialog. If two-factor authentication (2FA/TOTP) is enabled, also enter the 6-digit code.
  5. Confirm with “Delete permanently”. Your sessions on all devices end and you are signed out.

Technical note (for reviewers): the request goes to DELETE /api/auth/me/; the password is mandatory, and a TOTP code is mandatory as well when 2FA is enabled. Only the account owner can use this path — nobody who does not know your password (an education center manager or teacher, for example) can delete your account.

3. You can restore the account within 30 days

As soon as the deletion request is accepted, the account is disabled: sign-in is blocked and sessions on all devices are revoked. The data itself is not erased immediately — there is a 30-day restore window.

  • Restoring: within 30 days, enter the same phone number and password on the sign-in screen and the app offers to restore the account (the 2FA code is requested as well when enabled). After restoring, all of your data is still there.
  • After 30 days: a daily automated clean-up removes the account from the database permanently. After that, restoring is impossible.

4. What data is deleted

Once the 30-day window has passed, the following is permanently removed from the database:

  • Account and profile: phone number, name, username, email (if linked), password hash, avatar, 2FA (TOTP) secret, Telegram linkage, and profile preferences.
  • Learning activity: olympiad and test attempts, answers, scores, code submissions, essay grades, and AI analyses. This also removes you from the leaderboards.
  • Progress: coins, streaks, achievements, chosen rivals, and shop redemptions.
  • Notifications: in-app notifications and push subscriptions.
  • Messages: your AI assistant and admin support chat messages.
  • Organization links: your memberships in education centers.
  • Security records: login history (IP address, device string) and active sessions.
  • Subscription: premium/subscription records.

5. What may be retained

The following is retained for legal and security reasons, consistent with section 6 (“Data retention”) of our Privacy Policy:

  • Payment records: may be retained for accounting and dispute resolution as required by law. When the account is permanently deleted, the link between these records and your account is removed (the user field is cleared). Full card numbers are never stored on our servers.
  • Administrative audit log and blocked-IP list: kept for service security; the link to your account is removed from the audit entries.
  • Educational content you created: questions and olympiads you added as a teacher/manager may remain with the education center, but the author link is removed.

6. Limitations

  • Education center (organization) owners: if you own a center, the deletion request is rejected. Transfer center ownership to another user first, then delete the account. Email us if you need help with the transfer.
  • Platform administrators: for service security, a platform admin cannot delete their own account.
  • Blocked or already deleted accounts: in-app deletion does not work — please use the email path.

A parent or guardian may request access to, correction of, or full deletion of their child’s data (section 8 of the Privacy Policy).
